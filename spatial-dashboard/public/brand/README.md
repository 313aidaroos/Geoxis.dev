# Geoxis brand kit

Official Geoxis logo, cut out from Awad's original artwork (`logo-reference.png`, globe "G" mark + chrome "GeoXis" wordmark with a green X). The background was removed from the real pixels (rembg isnet mask + cleanup); nothing was redrawn or regenerated. Larger sizes are LANCZOS upscales of the cutout.

## Do

- Use the full-colour logo on dark surfaces (`#0f1117`–`#262b3d`).
- Use the white version on photos or busy/coloured backgrounds.
- Keep clear space around the logo of at least the height of the orb on the "i".
- Use `mark.png` when the space is square or under ~120px wide.

## Don't

- Don't redraw, recolour, regenerate or trace the logo (no SVG re-creations).
- Don't stretch, skew, rotate, or add shadows/outlines.
- Don't put the full-colour logo on white or light backgrounds; the chrome loses contrast. Use it on dark.
- Don't separate the green X from the wordmark or change its colour.
