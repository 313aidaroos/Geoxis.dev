Privacy Policy

Draft, pending review by Apixis Dev LLC. Last updated 2026-10-14.

Apixis Dev LLC runs Geoxis. This page explains what we collect.

What we collect. Your Apixis ID or email, the positions and assets you send, tracking numbers you look up, support messages, and payment records (card payments are handled by Stripe; we never see full card numbers).

Why. To run the map, bill your plan, answer support, and prevent abuse.

Who processes it. Supabase (database), Vercel (hosting), Stripe (card payments), Apixis Wallet (Ixis payments), Ship24 (package lookups), OpenSky Network and aisstream.io (public aircraft and ship positions), adsbdb, Planespotters.net and Wikimedia Commons (aircraft and ship identity and photos), and our AI provider for Cixy questions you ask.

Your choices. Ask for a copy or deletion of your data at geoxis@apixis.dev.
