Follow what is moving

A thing can be a plane, a ship, a truck, freight, mail, or equipment. You pick how many. Each one is $5 a month. The smallest plan is 3 things ($15 a month).

How it works

  1. Choose how many things you want on the map.
  2. Pay $5 a month for each one. That is 500 Ixis. Three things is the smallest plan.
  3. You can pay by card or with Ixis. 100 Ixis = $1. Card checkout is not set up yet, so the button pays with Ixis.
  4. Each payment covers 30 days. Pay again to keep those things on the map.
  5. Or start a 14-day free trial and follow 1 thing.
  6. A map report is $10 once (1,000 Ixis). For 200 or more things, email sales.
